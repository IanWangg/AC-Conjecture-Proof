# Andrews–Curtis research

Research target: ordinary (unstabilized) Andrews–Curtis triviality of

    AK(3) = <x,y | x^3 y^-4, xyx y^-1 x^-1 y^-1>.

## Status

No proof or counterexample is established by this repository. This is a research log; bounded search failures are not proofs of nontriviality, and proofs that the presented group is trivial are not AC trivializations.

The repository was empty when this investigation began on 2026-11-02. Findings and reproducible artifacts are recorded under `research/`, `experiments/` and `certificates/`. As of 2026-11-09 (Los Angeles time), after five rounds — fifteen notes A–O and a fifth, theory-focused round of twenty agents (TH01–TH20) — AK(3) remains open: none of the notes below trivializes it, obstructs it, or settles the AC conjecture. After round 4 the investigation was paused at the user's request and resumed for round 5 on 2026-11-09.

**Closing summary.** Established here with checked proofs or replayable certificates: uniform coordinate changes for the whole AK family (C: MS(n, y⁻¹x²y⁻¹) ~ AK(n); K: AK(n) ~ P_n = (xyxy⁻²x⁻¹y, xⁿy⁻¹x⁻¹y²), length n+11; G: a trefoil/T(2,2n+1) form), so AK(3) is AC-trivial iff P₃ (length 14) is; Aut(F₂) is AC-realized on the classes of AK(3), C3, P1 = C6 and P2; the published residual length-14 frontier is at most {AK3, P1 = C6, P2, C3, C4}, with fifteen further published residual components folded into known classes. Lower bounds for any AK(3) trivialization: ≥ 15 standard instructions, freely reduced total length ≥ 21 (E), cyclic total length ≥ 15 (J). No single generalized (ACM) move from AK(n), n ≥ 3, reaches a primitive relator (G); nor does one from P₃,…,P₈ with the relator c frozen (O). Round 5 extends the latter to all n ≥ 3 and, for P₃, P₄, P₅, to both relators (TH03). Where an obstruction could live is sharply constrained: no finite, solvable or virtually solvable quotient, no simple quotient, no quotient in which any class coordinate normally generates, obstruction always descends to the quotient by an abelian normal subgroup inside the derived subgroup or by a nonabelian minimal normal subgroup, so a minimal ("just-obstructing") quotient has neither (B, E, H; strengthened in round 5 to every central, abelian, solvable or finite normal subgroup — TH05, TH08); no monotone quantity (K); nothing attached to the presentation complex can detect "chirality" (N); and AK(3) is non-AC-trivial iff its AC distances along explicit finite 2-groups are unbounded (E). Exhaustive directed searches to cap 29 (2.9·10⁸ states) and exact orbit comparisons with the complete trivial side to length 22 found nothing (D, L, M). Two sharp open questions left by this work: whether the stabilizer of C4's class in GL₂(ℤ) is exactly D₆ (which would make C4 a counterexample) or infinite (N), and the two-pair abelian lifting problem (N §2.3). Corrections made during the project: D's Lemma D1(c) (the cyclic-product graph is directed), H's Theorem 2(ii) hypothesis, and K's remark on the group ⟨x,y | xyxy⁻²x⁻¹y⟩; each is recorded in a coordinator's review section at the end of the affected note.

Proves AK(n) ~AC (ψ((xy)ⁿx((yx)ⁿy)⁻¹), xyxYXY) for all n ≥ 1 — a form in which the trefoil and T(2,2n+1) roles swap (certificate for n = 3: 4,055 moves) — and that for n ≥ 3 no single ACM move from AK(n), with arbitrary conjugators or automorphisms, reaches a presentation with a primitive relator (for AK(2) one does, giving a 606-move trivialization). No AK(3) trivialization or obstruction.

- [M](research/research-m.md), the last affordable searches: a lean two-thread engine (validated byte-for-byte against D's at cap 26) completes AK(3)'s directed cyclic-product forward set to cap 29 — 286,729,520 states, no primitive relator, no contact with the trivial side T(20), only the 34 known benchmark members; cap 30 exceeds 5·10⁸ states and is inconclusive on this machine. Everything is engine-relative and directed, and is explicitly not evidence of non-triviality.

- [N](research/research-n.md), theory on the "chirality" question (is C4's AC class closed under Aut(F₂)? if not, the AC conjecture is false): proves that no invariant of the presentation complex (homeomorphism type, 3-deformation type, Q**-class, or any generalized-move class) can separate C4 from any automorphic image, so a chirality obstruction can live only in basis-sensitive quotients; reduces such quotients to ones with invariant kernel, extends H's lifting through nonabelian minimal normal subgroups to two pairs, and isolates the one open case (abelian normal subgroups inside the derived subgroup, for two merely normally generating pairs) as an explicit "affine monodromy" problem. By certificate: the stabilizer Ŝ(C4) ⊆ GL₂(ℤ) of C4's class contains a dihedral group D₆ of order 12, which is a maximal finite subgroup, so Ŝ(C4) is either exactly D₆ or infinite; none of the seven Nielsen-generator matrices lies in D₆, which explains I's negative searches. For X = T(2;4,9) the stabilizer is infinite (an infinite-order automorphism is realized by certificate). No AK(3) trivialization or obstruction.

- [O](research/research-o.md), structural: K's frozen relator c = xyxy⁻²x⁻¹y has y-exponent sum 0, and ⟨x,y | c⟩ ≅ F(a,b) ⋊_φ ℤ (a = x, b = yxy⁻¹, φ(a) = b, φ(b) = b⁻¹a) — a punctured-torus bundle group (recalled: the Gieseking manifold, double-covered by the figure-eight knot complement), so c-frozen generalized moves are exact twisted-conjugacy questions. Proves (Prop. O2) that a c-frozen move from P_n to a presentation with a primitive relator exists iff the killer xⁿy⁻¹x⁻¹y² is conjugate to xˢy in that group; exhibits such moves for n = 1, 2 (one-move trivializations of P₁ and P₂, certificates), and proves by an exact PSL(2,13) computation (coordinator re-verified) that none exists for n = 3,…,8 — the P₃-coordinate analogue of G's Proposition G2 for AK(3). Also corrects K: ⟨x,y | c⟩ is a 3-manifold group (just not a knot group). No AK(3) trivialization or obstruction.

*Obstruction side — where an obstruction can and cannot live*

- [TH05](research/research-th05.md) and, independently, [TH08](research/research-th08.md) §3: a quotient Q cannot obstruct U if some coordinate of some pair in the class has abelian or metabelian cokernel; hence **obstruction is insensitive to every central, abelian, solvable or finite normal subgroup of Q**. This removes the proviso of H's lifting theorem, closes H's open fibre-product case over arbitrary bases, and shows that a just-obstructing quotient has trivial centre and no abelian, solvable, finite or minimal normal subgroup (coordinator re-derived Theorems 1, 1⁺, 2 of TH05). TH05 also excludes just-non-solvable groups and, conditionally on externally reported theorems, SL_n(ℤ) (n ≥ 3) and SL₂(ℤ[1/p]).
- [TH10](research/research-th10.md): the same metabelian-cokernel filter applied to Baumslag–Solitar and torus class coordinates: a quotient obstructing AK(n) or any Miller–Schupp presentation must map y to an element of infinite order, so no periodic group (in particular no free Burnside group B(2,N)), no just-infinite group and no group with the normal subgroup property obstructs them; lists the characteristic quotients that survive all known filters (e.g. ⟨x,y | [x,y]ⁿ⟩ for n divisible by 3 or 4).
- [TH06](research/research-th06.md): with conjugation by an arbitrary element counted as one move, every pair with unimodular exponent matrix is within (Nielsen length + 8) moves of (x,y) in every nilpotent or abelian-by-nilpotent quotient and in the free pro-p group; so research-e's criterion is a statement about the *length of conjugators*, not the number of moves; the nilpotent chain stays faithful when only powers of generators are allowed as conjugators; a quasimorphism lower bound valid for arbitrary conjugators in F (coordinator re-derived the main theorems).
- [TH01](research/research-th01.md): the two-pair abelian lifting problem of research-n remains **open**; a substitution lemma giving corrections outside N's correction modules, lifting when the two normal closures commute, and a rigid-looking example that is not a counterexample.
- [TH12](research/research-th12.md): the "generator area" of AK(n) is exactly n+4 (x is a product of n+4 conjugates of relators and no fewer), so Dehn-function arguments cannot give more than logarithmic bounds here; nevertheless every trivialization of AK(n) by the 15 standard instructions needs at least n+2 multiplications and, for n ≥ 4, n−3 conjugations: **d_F(AK(n)) ≥ 2n−1**, the first lower bound in this repository that grows with n (a complexity statement, not an obstruction).

*Structure of the classes — symmetries, coordinates, exits*

- [TH15](research/research-th15.md): a short self-contained proof, for every n ≥ 1, that every automorphism of F₂ is AC-realized on AK(n) (Panteleev–Ushakov's theorem): AK(n) is one generalized move from the hub (x⁻¹y⁻⁽ⁿ⁺¹⁾xyⁿ, xyxy⁻¹x⁻¹y⁻¹), where the swap is inner modulo the braid relator and x ↦ xy is inner modulo the Baumslag–Solitar relator; 13 certificates (coordinator re-derived the proof). Makes D7, G1's Aut-form and K1.3 unconditional.
- [TH02](research/research-th02.md): "hidden reflections": a relator conjugate to (u²vⁿ)^{±1} is fixed by an automorphism that acts as the identity on its one-relator group, so that automorphism is AC-realized for every partner. This explains symbolically N's group D₆ ⊆ Ŝ(C4), the swap on AK(n) and the infinite stabilizer of X, and proves that the stabilizer of Y = T(4;7,5) is infinite; such reflections can never realize a Nielsen generator, and no element of Ŝ(C4) outside D₆ was found.
- [TH03](research/research-th03.md): exact invariants in the Gieseking coordinates (an integer-valued twisted trace from an explicit representation over ℤ[ω], a degree-4 trace and a mod-2 "mirror" invariant): **for every n ≥ 3 no generalized move with c frozen takes P_n to a presentation with a primitive relator**, and for P₃ (also P₄, P₅) none with the other relator frozen either; k_n is conjugate to xˢy iff n ∈ {1,2} and s = −2. The trace coincidences at n = 4, 5 were found independently by the coordinator's exact computation. A bounded census finds no two-move exit through short intermediate relators.
- [TH11](research/research-th11.md): Out of the Gieseking group is ℤ/2 and the stabilizer of the cyclic word c in Out(F₂) is generated by one explicit involution; complete invariants of twisted conjugacy modulo γ₅; a second, holonomy-free proof of the uniform one-move theorem; AK(3)'s class contains several "mirror pairs" of c-frozen classes (new certificates), so these invariants are not AC invariants.
- [TH04](research/research-th04.md): rigidity of the torus island: for n ≥ 3, with xⁿy⁻⁽ⁿ⁺¹⁾ frozen AK(n) has no torus-type or primitive generalized neighbour other than itself, and the same on the T(2,2n+1) sides of the other two island nodes; the third island node is exhibited for every n. The classification on the trefoil side is still missing, so the full three-orbit theorem is not proved.
- [TH07](research/research-th07.md): uniform classification of the first-exit states of AK(n) (4n(n+1)(2n+3) words); exits that change the braid relator cost total length ≥ 4n+3; but through a Baumslag–Solitar first-exit relator generalized paths reach presentations shorter than AK(n) at peak 2n+12 (n ≥ 5), so no (2+ε)n lower bound holds for generalized moves; new certified class members S_n and F_n.
- [TH13](research/research-th13.md): the two-parameter family AK(k;p) = (xᵖy⁻⁽ᵖ⁺¹⁾, (xy)ᵏx((yx)ᵏy)⁻¹): a duality generalizing G1, AK(3) ~AC ⟨x,y | x² = y³, (xy)³x = y(xy)³⟩ by plain AC moves, plateaus of unbounded depth, and Aut(F₂)-realization on AK(2;3), AK(2;4).
- [TH16](research/research-th16.md): metric small cancellation says nothing at these lengths; with displacement and Britton–Collins arguments instead, MS(n, x⁻¹y⁻¹x²y⁻¹) and its mirror are strict local minima of the generalized-move graph for every n ≥ 3, the second length-13 class Q is a local minimum, and P₃ is not (one move to a length-13 presentation).
- [TH17](research/research-th17.md): every T(2;p,q) is "the trefoil group with a killer": exact necklace dictionary, all coincidences (none with AK(n), n ≥ 2), a two-parameter family containing both AK(n) and T(2;·), and level rigidity under two restricted move classes; a uniform level shift would itself trivialize C3.

## Verification standard

A positive ordinary AC result must supply elementary relator moves and a replayable certificate, or a complete mathematical proof. Stabilization, changes of generators, and quotient rewriting must be explicitly justified rather than silently treated as elementary relator moves. An obstruction must be invariant under all permitted moves and distinguish the target from the standard tuple. Any computational exclusion states its move set and finite bounds.
