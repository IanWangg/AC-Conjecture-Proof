# Andrews–Curtis research

Research target: ordinary (unstabilized) Andrews–Curtis triviality of

    AK(3) = <x,y | x^3 y^-4, xyx y^-1 x^-1 y^-1>.

## Status

No proof or counterexample is established by this repository. This is a research log; bounded search failures are not proofs of nontriviality, and proofs that the presented group is trivial are not AC trivializations.

The repository was empty when this investigation began on 2026-11-02. Findings and reproducible artifacts are recorded under `research/`, `experiments/` and `certificates/`. As of 2026-11-03, after four rounds and fifteen research notes (A–O), AK(3) remains open: none of the notes below trivializes it, obstructs it, or settles the AC conjecture. The investigation was closed at the user's request after round 4.

**Closing summary.** Established here with checked proofs or replayable certificates: uniform coordinate changes for the whole AK family (C: MS(n, y⁻¹x²y⁻¹) ~ AK(n); K: AK(n) ~ P_n = (xyxy⁻²x⁻¹y, xⁿy⁻¹x⁻¹y²), length n+11; G: a trefoil/T(2,2n+1) form), so AK(3) is AC-trivial iff P₃ (length 14) is; Aut(F₂) is AC-realized on the classes of AK(3), C3, P1 = C6 and P2; the published residual length-14 frontier is at most {AK3, P1 = C6, P2, C3, C4}, with fifteen further published residual components folded into known classes. Lower bounds for any AK(3) trivialization: ≥ 15 standard instructions, freely reduced total length ≥ 21 (E), cyclic total length ≥ 15 (J). No single generalized (ACM) move from AK(3), P₃,…,P₈ reaches a primitive relator (G, O). Where an obstruction could live is sharply constrained: no finite, solvable or virtually solvable quotient, no simple quotient, no quotient in which any class coordinate normally generates, no quotient with an abelian normal subgroup inside its derived subgroup or a nonabelian minimal normal subgroup (B, E, H); no monotone quantity (K); nothing attached to the presentation complex can detect "chirality" (N); and AK(3) is non-AC-trivial iff its AC distances along explicit finite 2-groups are unbounded (E). Exhaustive directed searches to cap 29 (2.9·10⁸ states) and exact orbit comparisons with the complete trivial side to length 22 found nothing (D, L, M). Two sharp open questions left by this work: whether the stabilizer of C4's class in GL₂(ℤ) is exactly D₆ (which would make C4 a counterexample) or infinite (N), and the two-pair abelian lifting problem (N §2.3). Corrections made during the project: D's Lemma D1(c) (the cyclic-product graph is directed), H's Theorem 2(ii) hypothesis, and K's remark on the group ⟨x,y | xyxy⁻²x⁻¹y⟩; each is recorded in a coordinator's review section at the end of the affected note.

## Research notes

Round 1 (independent agents A–C):

- [A](research/research-a.md): every non-length-increasing quotient-conjugacy path from AK(n), n ≥ 3, stays on an explicit plateau, so an AC trivialization of AK(3) must reach cyclic total length ≥ 14.
- [B](research/research-b.md): explicit nilpotent-quotient certificates; AK(3) is non-AC-trivial iff its finite-quotient AC distances are unbounded.
- [C](research/research-c.md): uniform proof that MS(n, y⁻¹x²y⁻¹) is ordinary-AC-equivalent to AK(n) for all n ≥ 2.

Round 2 (independent agents D–F, each read round 1 first and chose its own direction):

- [D](research/research-d.md), constructive: 82 verified certificates, including 34 Miller–Schupp benchmark presentations and a second length-13 presentation AC-equivalent to AK(3), and AC realizations of the generators of Aut(F₂) on AK(3). The set of states forward-reachable from AK(3) by directed cyclic-product moves within total length 28 (69.2M states) has no primitive relator and matches none of the 1.43M published AC-trivial presentations (77,430 Aut(F₂)-orbits), even up to Aut(F₂). These are directed search bounds, not obstructions. **Correction (audit J):** D's Lemma D1(c) "every cyclic-product edge is reversible" is false — see the coordinator's review at the end of D's note — so D's "components" are forward-reachable sets and its separation bounds are for directed paths.
- [E](research/research-e.md), obstructions: AK(3) is AC-trivial iff its AC distances along an explicit chain of finite 2-groups stay bounded; no simple quotient and no quotient of ⟨x,y | x³=y⁴⟩ or B₃ can obstruct. Exhaustively, any trivialization by the 15 standard instructions needs ≥ 15 moves and passes through total freely reduced length ≥ 21.
- [F](research/research-f.md), other families: no Aut(F₂)-image of AK(n), of any MS(n,w), or of the trivial members of Johnson's P(m,n) is thickenable, so Lackenby's 2026 "thickenable ⇒ AC-trivial" theorem cannot apply to them directly. Every thickenable trivial-group class of length ≤ 16 is trivialized by strict descent.

Round 3 (independent agents G–L; each read all earlier notes first; G, H, I ran on Opus, J, K, L on Fable; all six are complete and coordinator-checked):

- [L](research/research-l.md), computational calibration: computes the complete set T(20) of presentations forward-reachable from (x,y) by cyclic products within total length 20 (22.2M states, 1.81M Aut(F₂)-orbits) and shows that no state of AK(3)'s cap-28 forward set is an automorphic image of any of them (an exact, not sampled, meet-in-the-middle). Classifies every unimodular pair of total length 13 (all are AC-trivial with peak ≤ 18 or in AK(3)'s class) and tabulates the "barrier spectrum" at lengths 14–16: the farthest resolved trivial presentations at length 14 need peak 26, while AK(3) and the other frontier classes are unresolved at peaks ≥ 26–28. Independently found the Lemma D1(c) error. Eight independent elementary certificates for Miller–Schupp benchmark presentations that Shehper et al. list as unsolved (already reported solved, without ledgers, by Fagan et al.). Feasibility estimate: each +2 in search cap costs ×6.7 in states; cap 30 (≈9 GB) and cap 32 (≈60 GB) are the last affordable AK(3) searches.

- [H](research/research-h.md), obstructions: a quotient in which some coordinate of some pair AC-equivalent to U normally generates cannot obstruct U; obstruction lifts through abelian normal subgroups (modulo their intersection with the derived subgroup) and through nonabelian minimal normal subgroups, so no virtually solvable quotient obstructs anything; U is non-AC-trivial iff some "just-obstructing" quotient exists, and such a quotient has no abelian normal subgroup inside its derived subgroup, no nonabelian minimal normal subgroup and no monolith; the abelian fibre-product family G ×_{G_ab} ℤ² carries no invariant. A coordinator's review records one too-strong clause in Theorem 2(ii) (consequences unaffected).
- [G](research/research-g.md), constructive via the 3-manifold locus: makes generalized (ACM) moves with a frozen torus-knot relator exact (conjugacy in ⟨x,y | xᵖyᑫ⟩ is decidable with arbitrary conjugators) and compiles them to certificates. Proves AK(n) ~AC (ψ((xy)ⁿx((yx)ⁿy)⁻¹), xyxYXY) for all n ≥ 1 — a form in which the trefoil and T(2,2n+1) roles swap (certificate for n = 3: 4,055 moves) — and that for n ≥ 3 no single ACM move from AK(n), with arbitrary conjugators or automorphisms, reaches a presentation with a primitive relator (for AK(2) one does, giving a 606-move trivialization). Within its bounds AK(3)'s torus-type states form a rigid 3-orbit island with no primitive or meridional exit. Also shows, by explicit certificates, that two pairs of residual length-15/16 classes (R327 with C3; a member of R74 with one of R62) are AC-equivalent up to an explicit automorphism, so their triviality questions coincide. No AK(3) trivialization or obstruction.
- [I](research/research-i.md), other families and the residual frontier: 33 verified certificates. P1 ~AC C6 (so the six published residual length-14 classes are at most five: AK3, P1 = C6, P2, C3, C4); five published residual components (R212 → AK(3), R327 → C3, R28 → P1, R82 and R86 → P2) are folded into the frontier classes by plain AC moves, and five more (R22, R69, R111, R114, R223) into one class R9; Aut(F₂) is AC-realized on the classes of C3, P1 and P2, so automorphism-orbit matches into these classes are genuine AC equivalences. Uniform results on the family T(a;p,q) = (xᵖyᑫ, xᵃyx⁻¹y): an exact triviality criterion (Prop. G), a one-parameter equivalence T(a;1+mk,q) ~AC T(a;mk+m−1,q+2) with m = a+1 (Lemma S, 2,898 generated certificates), and "a torus relator x^m y^n with n ≡ ±1 (mod m) whose partner has syllable length 2 is already AC-trivial by one explicit move" (Prop. M), which makes any defect-2 state found in AK(3)'s class an immediate trivialization. Observation: C4 (and two relatives) contain no automorphic image of themselves within the searched caps, unlike AK3, C3, P1, P2 — a proof that C4 is not AC-equivalent to one of its own letter-permuted images would disprove the AC conjecture (Prop. C), but no such proof exists.
- [K](research/research-k.md), theory: proves for every n ≥ 2 that AK(n) is ordinary-AC-equivalent to P_n = (xyxy⁻²x⁻¹y, xⁿy⁻¹x⁻¹y²) by 4n−1 cyclic products (certificates for n = 3–7, 10; symbolic identities re-derived by the coordinator). |P_n| = n+11, so every AK(n) with n ≥ 5 is AC-equivalent to a strictly shorter presentation, and the whole AK series becomes the killers xⁿ·y⁻¹x⁻¹y² of the single one-relator group ⟨x,y | xyxy⁻²x⁻¹y⟩; AK(3) is AC-trivial iff P_3 = (xyxYYXy, xxxYXyy) is. Also closes two proposed programs: no quantity that differs between AK(2) and (x,y) can be monotone along any complete move class, and E's "uniform correction cost" grows only logarithmically and is otherwise a restatement of the problem. Shehper et al. report a one-parameter family with the same length-reducibility consequence, so novelty is not established.
- [J](research/research-j.md), audit and strategy: proof-checked A–F; found the Lemma D1(c) error above (everything else checked out); proved that the first exit from A's plateau has total cyclic length ≥ 2n+9, i.e. any AC trivialization of AK(3) reaches cyclic length ≥ 15 (A: 14); ranked strategic advice in its §7.

Round 4 (Fable agents M, N, O; each read all earlier notes first; all three complete and coordinator-checked):

- [M](research/research-m.md), the last affordable searches: a lean two-thread engine (validated byte-for-byte against D's at cap 26) completes AK(3)'s directed cyclic-product forward set to cap 29 — 286,729,520 states, no primitive relator, no contact with the trivial side T(20), only the 34 known benchmark members; cap 30 exceeds 5·10⁸ states and is inconclusive on this machine. The trivial side is raised to T(22) (169.4M states, 14.66M Aut-orbits) and the exact automorphism-orbit meet-in-the-middle at (29, 22) finds no common orbit, nor do P1 = C6, P2, C4, Y at (28, 22). C4 is complete to cap 34 (42.9M states): no primitive relator, no trivial-side contact, and still no automorphic image of itself. All 6,741 complex classes of length ≤ 20 in AK(3)'s cap-29 set are non-thickenable. One certificate (a Nielsen generator realized on C6's class). Everything is engine-relative and directed, and is explicitly not evidence of non-triviality.

- [N](research/research-n.md), theory on the "chirality" question (is C4's AC class closed under Aut(F₂)? if not, the AC conjecture is false): proves that no invariant of the presentation complex (homeomorphism type, 3-deformation type, Q**-class, or any generalized-move class) can separate C4 from any automorphic image, so a chirality obstruction can live only in basis-sensitive quotients; reduces such quotients to ones with invariant kernel, extends H's lifting through nonabelian minimal normal subgroups to two pairs, and isolates the one open case (abelian normal subgroups inside the derived subgroup, for two merely normally generating pairs) as an explicit "affine monodromy" problem. By certificate: the stabilizer Ŝ(C4) ⊆ GL₂(ℤ) of C4's class contains a dihedral group D₆ of order 12, which is a maximal finite subgroup, so Ŝ(C4) is either exactly D₆ or infinite; none of the seven Nielsen-generator matrices lies in D₆, which explains I's negative searches. For X = T(2;4,9) the stabilizer is infinite (an infinite-order automorphism is realized by certificate). No AK(3) trivialization or obstruction.

- [O](research/research-o.md), structural: K's frozen relator c = xyxy⁻²x⁻¹y has y-exponent sum 0, and ⟨x,y | c⟩ ≅ F(a,b) ⋊_φ ℤ (a = x, b = yxy⁻¹, φ(a) = b, φ(b) = b⁻¹a) — a punctured-torus bundle group (recalled: the Gieseking manifold, double-covered by the figure-eight knot complement), so c-frozen generalized moves are exact twisted-conjugacy questions. Proves (Prop. O2) that a c-frozen move from P_n to a presentation with a primitive relator exists iff the killer xⁿy⁻¹x⁻¹y² is conjugate to xˢy in that group; exhibits such moves for n = 1, 2 (one-move trivializations of P₁ and P₂, certificates), and proves by an exact PSL(2,13) computation (coordinator re-verified) that none exists for n = 3,…,8 — the P₃-coordinate analogue of G's Proposition G2 for AK(3). Also corrects K: ⟨x,y | c⟩ is a 3-manifold group (just not a knot group). No AK(3) trivialization or obstruction.

Cross-check: D and E independently found the same second length-13 class in AK(3)'s AC class, (x²yx⁻¹y⁻², x³y⁻²x⁻¹y) up to signed generator permutation. All 89 strict ordinary-AC certificates pass `python3 experiments/elementary_verify.py <file>`. Two files use other formats: `certificates/ms3_to_ak3.json` (C's packaged certificate, checked by `python3 experiments/ms_ak_family.py --verify certificates/ms3_to_ak3.json`) and `certificates/e_ak3_quotient_paths.json` (quotient-only paths, explicitly **not** AC trivializations).

## Verification standard

A positive ordinary AC result must supply elementary relator moves and a replayable certificate, or a complete mathematical proof. Stabilization, changes of generators, and quotient rewriting must be explicitly justified rather than silently treated as elementary relator moves. An obstruction must be invariant under all permitted moves and distinguish the target from the standard tuple. Any computational exclusion states its move set and finite bounds.
