# Round 13 stable AC proof track

A change of ambient marking shortens the strict donor-release path to cyclic peak **30**. The new marking gives a chart whose rank-two projection is absent from the generated ordinary cap-32 catalogue. A separate two-donor completion constructs another primitive chart using both retained relators. Neither chart leads to a basis in the checked searches. [Receipts](../results/round13_stable_proof_whitehead.json).

## Remarking and strict projections

The Round 12 donor-release endpoint has cyclic energy 35. Two joint Whitehead reductions of all three relators lower the energy to 30 and compose to the marking $x\mapsto x$, $y\mapsto y$, $z\mapsto y^{-1}x^{-1}zx$. Transporting the full original path by that map gives the new endpoint `(XYZyxzxy, XYYXzyxYXZy, XYZxyyZxzxy)`. The marking is compiled on the initial stabilization before transporting the moves, giving **397** strict rank-three instructions, raw peak 42 and cyclic peak 30. [Original path](round-12-stable-proof.md), [marked release ledger](../certificates/round13_stable_proof_remasked_release.json).

Starting at the remasked endpoint, a strict path of **1,137** rank-three moves from stabilized AK(3) has the same cyclic peak 30 and reaches `(XYzYZYXYzy, XYxyzyZyxZ, XYxzyZY)`. Whitehead descent of the first relator makes it a primitive deletion chart. Compiling that chart and clearing the other rows yields the rank-two tuple `(XXYxYXXYxxy, XXYXYxxyXyxxy)`, of cyclic energy 24. Its signed key `(xxyXXyxyXXY, xxyXyXXYxxYXY)` is absent from both ordinary inputs. The complete strict stable ledger uses **18,477** instructions, maximum rank three, raw peak 234 and cyclic peak **170**. The prefix peak applies to reaching the rank-three chart. [Strict rank-three prefix](../certificates/round13_stable_proof_external_prefix.json), [complete stable ledger](../certificates/round13_stable_proof_external_projection.jsonl), [ordinary catalogue provenance](round-11-cyclic32.md).

## Product menus in the new marking

The fixed-marking runs follow only nonprimitive rank-three states from the remasked release root and harvest each first charted exit. After 40,000 expansions the cap-31 run reaches 1,976,689 chartless orbits and gives 55,431 signed projections. The cap-32 run reaches 4,080,631 chartless orbits and gives 66,715 signed projections. Neither run closes its corridor. Both retain all bounded products examined at their expanded sources.

A separate beam interleaves cyclic products with greedy joint Whitehead descent. It tests every distinct product at each expanded marking for primitive quotient coordinates before any pruning. The product cap is 35 with at most five additional units per source, the normalized energy cutoff is 32 and the width is 128. The heuristic selects one descent representative and need not traverse its level Whitehead orbit. Six layers examine 127,394 distinct product orbits and 57,934 marked projected pairs, without a primitive quotient. The positive control from the independently trivial geometric source hits in one product, and a separate literal replay verifies the hit. [Native beam](../experiments/round13_stable_proof_whitehead.cpp), [normalization and membership checks](../experiments/round13_stable_proof_whitehead.py).

The three searches together give 45,511 signed rank-two projection orbits through energy 36, with zero exact signed orbit matches against the 23,902 verified archive rows. There are 10,263 projections above energy 32. Of the 35,248 through energy 32, 33,364 occur already at cap 31 and 1,816 first occur in the cap-32 addition catalogue. Exactly 68 are absent from both, including four at energy 23. Arbitrary-tail subgroup indexing against the 21,426 archive pairs of energy 20 through 56 gives no joins. The check against the remaining 2,476 shorter archive pairs is in progress. ## A chart using both donors

Fix the other two relators and fold a bouquet generated by the receiver and based conjugates of the fixed donors. At each of two completion stages insert every cyclic rotation of both signed donors at every old vertex and fold. For any resulting loop $w$, the determinant of its exponent row with the two fixed exponent rows, divided by the original marked determinant, gives its exponent in the cyclic subgroup generated by the receiver in the quotient by both donors. Weight $\pm1$ makes $w$ a valid recipient of a fixed-donor Andrews–Curtis block. We use the shortest reduced weighted return over the other two letters after choosing an oriented edge of the proposed deleted generator, through width ten. This is a search for literal chart words in the finite graphs.

The search finds 115 combinations of receiving slot and signed cyclic chart, including 83 whose abelian basis coordinates require both donors. The selected word `zyxYYXXY` has basis coordinates $(-1,-1,-1)$. In a sequence acting only on the receiver, using just one donor would leave its coefficient on the unused donor zero. The annotated fold verifies a five-term expression in the free group and compiles four donor factors to 87 elementary moves. Prepending the remasked release route gives a strict **484**-move rank-three path, at cyclic peak **38**. The literal deletion of $z$ projects to `(XXYxyXyxYY, XXXYYXyxxyXyxYY)` in **1,150** strict moves with full cyclic peak 77. [Two-donor search and compiler](../experiments/round13_stable_proof_two_donor.py), [rank-three ledger](../certificates/round13_stable_proof_two_donor_prefix.json), [strict projection](../certificates/round13_stable_proof_two_donor_projection.jsonl).

## Direct clearing of the literal two-donor chart

The same chart can be cleared locally from its verified prefix, without transporting the entire path by a new ambient marking. Write its pivot as $zw$ with `w=yxYYXXY`. Multiplying the other rows by explicit conjugates of the pivot replaces each $z$ with $w^{-1}$ and each $z^{-1}$ with $w$. This leaves the displayed two projected rows and the pivot $zw$.

A finite word search uses cyclic cuts $r=uv^{-1}$ of the two projected rows and their inverses. The replacement $aub\to avb$ is right multiplication by a conjugate of $r^{-1}$. The search reduces $w$ to the empty word using **20** such factors with intermediate free length at most **14**. Applying those factors to $zw$ leaves $z$ alone, allowing immediate destabilization. The independent native replay reads **1,050** strict instructions, maximum rank three, raw peak **58**, and cyclic peak **40**, down from 77 for the global-marking compiler. [Local compiler](../experiments/round13_stable_proof_local_clear.py), [factor and peak receipt](../results/round13_stable_proof_local_clear.json), [strict local ledger](../certificates/round13_stable_proof_local_clear.jsonl).

## Reproduction

~~~sh
mkdir -p "$work"
c++ -O3 -std=c++17 experiments/round12_stable_proof_corridor.cpp -o "$work/corridor"
c++ -O3 -std=c++17 experiments/round13_stable_proof_whitehead.cpp -o "$work/whitehead"
for cap in 31 32; do
  "$work/corridor" 3 "$cap" 40000 XYZyxzxy XYYXzyxYXZy XYZxyyZxzxy "$work/min$cap"
done
"$work/whitehead" 35 32 6 128 XXYxyzXyxYZY XYxZyxYxyzy XYxZyxZYxyzy "$work/beam35"
python3 -B experiments/round13_stable_proof_whitehead.py collect "$work/projected36.tsv" \
    "$reverse" 36 "$work/min31.pairs.tsv" "$work/min32.pairs.tsv" "$work/beam35.pairs.tsv"
python3 -B experiments/round13_stable_proof_whitehead.py coverage "$work/projected36.tsv" \
    "$cap31" "$outside32" > "$work/coverage32.json"
python3 -B experiments/round13_stable_proof_whitehead.py remask \
    certificates/round12_stable_proof_corridor_donor_release.json "$work/remasked.json"
python3 -B experiments/round13_stable_proof_whitehead.py lift "$work/remasked.json" \
    "$work/min31.nodes.tsv" "$work/min31.pairs.tsv" "$work/coverage32.json" \
    "$work/external.jsonl" "$work/external-prefix.json"
python3 -B experiments/round13_stable_proof_two_donor.py "$work/remasked.json" 2 \
    "$work/two-donor.json" --both-donors
python3 -B experiments/round09_stable_proof_rank_three_chart.py "$work/two-donor.json" 2 \
    "$work/two-donor.jsonl" --literal z
python3 -B experiments/round13_stable_proof_local_clear.py \
    certificates/round13_stable_proof_two_donor_prefix.json 2 z "$work/local-clear.jsonl"
~~~

Use the verified rank-two reverse archive as `reverse` and the complete generated ordinary sources as `cap31` and `outside32`. The native strict verifier checks both JSONL paths.
