# Four conjugations necessary for an ordinary AK(3) trivialization

2026-11-19. Every ordinary rank-two AC path from literal AK(3) to a free basis uses at least **four single-generator conjugations**. Round 06 excludes fewer than three. Round 11 reduces the three-conjugation case for reaching a basis to the actual signed letter sequence $x,x,Y$. The proof below shows that this sequence cannot even expose a primitive, with arbitrary Nielsen preludes, interleaves and suffixes and regardless of the receiving rows. The conjugation bound is for ordinary rank two. The argument leaves stable paths with extra rows open. [Round 06 conjugation census](../results/round06_stable_disproof_conjugations.json), [Round 11 receiving-row census](../results/round11_three_conjugations.json), [exact fibre product and voltage checks](../results/round18_ac_disproof_mixed_phases.json).

## The last ordinary schedule with three conjugations

Write $H=\langle p,b\rangle$ for the literal AK(3) subgroup and use $H^x=xHx^{-1}$. Immediately before the first conjugation, both rows lie in $H$. Let $u$ be the row that stays fixed and let $v$ receive the first $x$. The resulting tuple lies in $A=\langle u,xvx^{-1}\rangle$. The second $x$, applied to either row after arbitrary Nielsen operations, leaves both rows in $\langle A,xAx^{-1}\rangle\le\langle u,H^x,H^{x^2}\rangle$. Set $J=\langle H^x,H^{x^2}\rangle$. After the final $Y$ and its Nielsen suffix the rows lie in the normal closure of $\langle J,u\rangle$ in $K_u=\langle J,Y,u\rangle$. The argument uses the actual conjugating letters without a change of common marking.

Set $B=\langle J,Y\rangle$. Its rank-five folded core has 16 vertices. The replayer gives an integer character $\chi$ of $B$ killing $J$ and sending $Y$ to one, and excludes ambient primitives from its kernel. Form the component of the pair of based vertices in the fibre product of the folded cores of $H$ and $B$. Its 12 vertices represent simultaneous reads in both graphs. The pullback of $\chi$ to this component is an exact coboundary. Write $f(a,b)$ for its height at source vertex $a$ and overgroup vertex $b$. The replay checks every transition and verifies that the heights of distinct source vertices over any one overgroup vertex are distinct.

Read the maximal prefix of a reduced word $u\in H$ and its maximal inverse suffix in the core of $B$. Their simultaneous endpoints in the fibre product are $(a,b)$ and $(c,d)$. If $u\in B$, its character vanishes by the coboundary check. If the reads meet and $u\notin B$, split them at the same position so $a=c$ and $b\ne d$. There are exactly two unordered pairs of distinct fibre vertices with the same source vertex. Their representative words are the two original AK(3) relators $p,b$. Each requires one vertex identification in the core of $B$. The resulting characters kill the four generators of $J$ and the representative word and send $Y$ to one. Their restrictions to $B$ equal $\chi$ on its marked generators. The value on an arbitrary word with the same split endpoints therefore agrees with the value on its representative, because the two prefix and suffix heights are determined by the fibre endpoints. Both enlarged kernels exclude primitives.

If the reads leave a nonempty gap $g$, attach its path between $b$ and $d$. The first and reversed last labels of this path are missing in the core of $B$ at their respective endpoints. Extend the character by assigning the new path total voltage $f(c,d)-f(a,b)$. This kills $u$. An ambient cyclic primitive uses a signed alphabet and avoids the double of one of its two letters. The replayer checks reachability in each of the eight corresponding languages in the source graph. From every fibre state it permits a first move only when its label is missing from the overgroup at that endpoint. It finds no nonempty path ending at any fibre state with the reversed last label missing there, allowing equal as well as distinct overgroup endpoints.

With distinct endpoints the gap is an immersed path. With equal endpoints and distinct first and reversed last labels it is a circle attached directly to the old core. In either case the interior vertices have degree two. A cyclically reduced loop using the new edges must traverse the entire gap in one direction, even if it also uses old edges. The signed double-free check excludes this for a primitive. In the remaining case the inserted gap folds to a circle on a nonempty stem. A cyclic primitive loop cannot cross the stem and return, since that would use both signs of the same generator. A cyclically reduced loop staying in the new circle is a nonzero power of the cyclic reduction of $g$. Its voltage can vanish only if $f(c,b)=f(a,b)$. The distinct-height check then gives $a=c$, so $g$ was already a loop in the source graph at that vertex. Its cyclic reduction cannot be primitive because $H$ is primitive-free. The primitive exclusion in the kernel of $\chi$ handles loops that stay in the old core. This proves the stated normal-closure exclusion for every $u\in H$. [Source primitive exclusion](round-09-rank-two-overgroups.md), [gap folding](round-09-rank-two-overgroups.md#adjoining-a-single-outside-word).

This strengthens the Round 17 calibration. There, replacing both outer coordinates by whole copies of $H$ gave a literal ambient rose for $x,x,Y$. Keeping the first stationary element $u$ in the calculation is sufficient even when it is an arbitrary word of $H$. [Prior full-rose calculation](round-17-ac-disproof.md#literal-ak3-calibration).

## Mixed first words at the external energy-24 pair

For the E24 pair a separate exact calculation excludes three phases when the first relative word is gapless in its source core and the second relative word is any signed generator. The third relative word is arbitrary. It also covers all reduced first words of length at most three, including the 34 that have an unread gap. The Nielsen operations are unrestricted. E24 is ordinarily reachable from AK(3), with exact ordinary cyclic entry peak 37. This is a local phase obstruction at a pair in the ordinary AK(3) component. [Ordinary entry and exact peak](round-17-cyclic-peak-gap.md).

For first words $t,s$, the pre-final subgroup is contained in $L=\langle H,H^t,H^s,H^{st}\rangle$, using $H$ for the E24 source in this section. The normal-closure test is in $\langle L,r\rangle$ with arbitrary $r$. If $t=a q b$ with $a,b\in H$ for a source path difference $q$, common conjugation by $a^{-1}$ makes the first two-copy group $J_q=\langle H,H^q\rangle$. The normalized second word belongs to $J_q s J_q$ since $a\in H$. Modifying the second word by such double-coset factors conjugates the envelope, so the same signed letter may be used in the check. Both orientations of each first path pair are included. Changing $s$ to $s^{-1}$ conjugates the envelope, so positive $x,y$ suffice for the second letter. The first and second word conditions, and the unrestricted third word, are understood in the common-conjugation gauges for these three phases.

The two-phase theorem gives primitive exclusion in $L$, since $L$ lies in the normal closure of $J_t$ in $\langle J_t,s\rangle$. As in Round 17, the third word either already lies in $L$, adds an unread gap excluded by a new chord character, or adjoins by a vertex identification. In the last case the replayer projects the identified vertices to the cumulative graph of $\langle H,t,s\rangle$. If the resulting cumulative graph is proper, Round 13 excludes primitives in the source normal closure. At an ambient cumulative graph it checks the actual enlargement of $L$. The weighted fold tracks differences of vertex heights on the original core of $L$. Identifying vertices $a,b$ imposes $f(b)-f(a)=-1$, so the adjoined word $p_a p_b^{-1}$ has voltage one. When a fold equates two edges with the same label, equal edge voltages force the corresponding height difference on their endpoints. Weighted union-find propagates these equations and takes the gcd of any inconsistencies. The folded graph gives the corresponding cyclic quotient because adjoining one word makes the quotient by the source normal closure cyclic, generated by that word. The checker independently verifies the folded transition table, pullback coboundary, inverse voltages and the normalization on the new word. All E24 character exclusions here use an infinite cyclic quotient. The strict potential inequalities and the balanced-gap refinement exclude cyclic primitive loops at voltage zero. A separate order-three quotient control has an explicit primitive in its kernel, and literal AK(3)'s full-rose envelope gives quotient order one.
