# Research round 01

2026-11-19. The five targets remain open in this project. This round produced finite search exclusions, two descent arguments for stable paths, and a reduction of stable counterexample searches to ordinary distances at fixed padded ranks.

Five agents took the five targets. The workspace supplies three simultaneous researcher slots alongside the coordinator, so the assignments ran in overlapping waves. The ranks, moves and endpoints use the marked free-group convention in the competition definitions. The earlier published claim of stable AK(3) triviality relied on an incorrect theorem. ## What the tracks establish together

- The archive supplies exact ordinary starting points for the disjoint-axis argument. Both energy-20 pairs outside the earlier cap-22 component pass the stronger voltage-kernel exclusion. Starting from either pair, arbitrary Nielsen mixing, one independent conjugation phase and an arbitrary Nielsen suffix cannot produce a primitive. Further alternating phases remain open.
- Geometric descent and ordinary pivot projection give an end-to-end certificate criterion. A geometric rank-three waypoint reached from the one-pair stabilization of AK(3), through continuously pivoted or explicitly excisable segments, implies ordinary rank-two triviality. This uses Lackenby's rank-preserving theorem for thickenable presentations. [Lackenby, Theorems 1.3 and 6.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.06122v1).
- We applied the geometric test to all 3,675 AK(3) archive waypoints. Eight rank-two Nielsen substitutions suffice for the length-decreasing Whitehead steps on cyclic conjugacy classes, since signed permutations and simultaneous inner conjugation preserve cyclic length. The implementation freely and cyclically reduces each image and independently checks every inverse-separating cut in the resulting minimal graph. There were 2,051 strict shortenings and 3,575 distinct minimized pair keys. All 3,575 parity contradictions replayed in the independent checker. The pairs have unimodular exponent matrices and minimum total length greater than two, so they are disk-busting by the rank-two splitting argument. Zieschang's minimal-diagram theorem excludes geometricity of these multiclasses in every basis. For reproduction, replay the archive with cap 210 and pass its forward.tsv to experiments/round01_geometric_archive.py. [Geometric descent and references](round-01-stable-proof.md), [full screen](../results/round01_geometric_full_archive.json), [screen through energy 32](../results/round01_geometric_archive.json).
- The coordinator's [finite Magnus tower](round-01-magnus-distance.md) detects all bounded stable paths at an explicit truncation degree, including exact checks on the deleted singleton and surviving rows. Combining it with peak-rank normalization reduces a stable counterexample to divergence of ordinary quotient distances for every fixed padding rank. The thresholds can be proved separately for different ranks. The degree-five experiment uses that reduction to rule out a stable shortcut at any rank.

## Questions for round 02

1. **AK(3) trivialization.** Start from the certified outside-cap waypoints. Compute exact second-phase candidates and compare their subgroup cores with the known-trivial archive. Reuse the geometric screen where the literal diagrams become tractable.
2. **Stable AC proof.** Extend geometric descent to families of auxiliary generators that can be eliminated as a succession of primitive equations. Determine which changes of pivot system can connect two such sections.
3. **Ordinary AC proof.** Generalize the continuous-pivot compilation to multiple independently solvable auxiliary generators. Test which pivotless gaps can be removed with the available Magnus conjugacy criterion.
4. **Ordinary AC disproof.** Use the certified barbell kernels and the two low-energy archive escape subgroups as test cases. Search for a uniform condition on long bridges or a certificate marking a sharp failure of the voltage method.
5. **Stable AC disproof.** Use the three-crossing condition to constrain optimizing padded paths. Test whether mod-two row spans, higher Magnus degree, or the pivot projection lemma impose stronger conditions when a cut has exactly three crossings.
