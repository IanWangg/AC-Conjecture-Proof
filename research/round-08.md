## Joint results

- The independent native audit proves that the complete ordinary cap-30 component consists of 73,876,424 signed orbits and contains no primitive coordinate. Any ordinary trivialization must attain cyclic peak at least **31**. The constructive track shows that the next cap already gives access to new marked nonprimitive endpoints. [Full component audit](round-08-cyclic30.md), [strict cap-31 certificates](round-08-ak3.md).
- Exact free-word calculations exclude every ordinary or stable path with at most 18 instructions, with no word-length restriction. The degree-ten Magnus quotient also excludes ordinary paths of that length. Any stable path of length 18 in the quotient would have to use exactly one added pair. In degree nine every shortest stable path has rank two throughout. [Free-group distance](round-08-free-distance.md), [Magnus distances and independent replay](round-08-magnus-distance.md).
- Native generation of the full cap-22 rank-three search reached 246,052,265 signed orbits. All 432 rank-two reentries lie in the independently verified ordinary cap-22 component. This gives a generated exclusion at peak ranks at most three, with independent rank-three closure verification still running. [Generation and return checks](round-08-stable-proof.md).
- Changing both donor rows can realize chart switches ruled out by a one-donor obstruction. The theorem requires a common free complement for the exposed primitives. The first two layers of the new rank-three scan give concrete charts on which to test this hypothesis and other ordinary ancestries. [Donor criterion and controls](round-08-ac-proof.md), [chart and block census](round-08-stable-proof.md).
- Ordinary equivalence to a generating tuple lifts through any solvable normal kernel contained in the derived group, in every finite rank. The rank-three control in a nonsolvable ambient group uses a genuine conjugation after quotient-level donor corrections. This covers finite solvable-kernel extensions of the quotients previously considered. [Lifting argument and 128-instruction control](round-08-solvable-kernels.md).

## Questions for round 09

1. **AK(3) trivialization.** Track the complete cap-31 frontier and search for primitive or spherical suffixes from the exact new entries. Combine short completion with changing both receivers and audit every candidate in the free group.
2. **Stable AC proof.** Finish the independent cap-22 closure and return checks. Compile ambient changes of rank-three deletion charts using ordinary moves in the once-stabilized orbit and try the resulting paths toward a standard basis.
3. **Ordinary AC proof.** Test simultaneous donor correction on actual failing chart switches and seek weaker hypotheses than a shared free complement. Identify when quotient-generation data alone can produce one.
4. **Ordinary AC disproof.** Replace the unique-lobe condition by a criterion that explicitly tracks nested cyclic reads after repeated Nielsen rebasing. Test whether folding finite collars at bridge attachments preserves a separating edge when overlap projections have repeated vertices.
5. **Stable AC disproof.** Complete the one-padding degree-nine retraction at middle budget 16, the only remaining case for degree-ten stable distance at least 19. Use the signed product counts and necessary original-letter conjugations to restrict the next exact free-group search.
