# Same-rank overgroups of AK(3) and elementary substitution factors

2026-11-19. The subgroups generated by the original marked AK(3) relators and by the new energy-17 pair each have exactly two rank-two overgroups in $F(x,y)$: the subgroup itself and $F(x,y)$. Both proper subgroups are primitive-free. The marked endomorphisms defined by the relator images therefore cannot be written as compositions of automorphisms and the single-generator substitutions examined in the stable proof track. [Two independent quotient folds and exact primitive screens](../results/round09_rank_two_overgroups.json).

## Complete finite overgroup census

Let $H\le K\le F$ be finitely generated subgroups, with $H$ mapping onto the abelianization of the ambient free group $F$ of rank $n$, and with $\operatorname{rank}K=n$. The image subgraph of the based Stallings core of $H$ in the core of $K$ is connected and carries the loops defining $H$. Its fundamental group is a free factor of $K$ by extending a spanning tree, and has rank at least $n$ by the abelianization assumption. It therefore carries all of $K$. Since the based core contains only edges on reduced based loops, the image is the entire based core of $K$. This realizes $K$ by vertex identifications and their forced folds. Iterate the identification of every vertex pair on every discovered graph, deduplicating by its rooted labelled adjacency array. A quotient realizing $K$ is reached by applying the identifications in its fiber partition one by one. [Miasnikov, Ventura and Weil on principal overgroups](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0610880).

For $H_0=\langle x^3y^{-4},xyxy^{-1}x^{-1}y^{-1}\rangle$, the complete search produces 82 quotients, distributed over ranks two through five as $2,21,47,12$. Two independent folding implementations agree on all 1,625 tested single identifications. The only rank-two quotients are the ten-vertex core of $H_0$ and the ambient rose. The finite partial-map criterion excludes every ambient primitive in $H_0$. The [new energy-17 pair](round-09-cyclic31.md) independently has just its own core and the ambient rose at rank two, among 1,839 principal quotients and 72,097 checked identifications. Its own core also contains no ambient primitive.

For a calibration, compose $x\mapsto x^2yx^{-1}$ fixing $y$ with $y\mapsto y^2xy^{-1}$ fixing $x$, with the first map applied to the images under the second. Both have unimodular abelianization and both have proper image. The census recovers the actual intermediate rank-two subgroup containing $y$. Its three rank-two overgroups include its own core, that intermediate core and the ambient rose.

## Adjoining a single outside word

For a reduced word $q$, read its maximal prefix and its maximal inverse suffix from the base of the core of $H$. If those reads leave a nonempty gap, attach the gap as a path between their endpoints. Its first and reversed last directions are absent at those endpoints. When the two endpoints coincide, the new path may fold into a stem and a cyclic circuit, retaining a nonempty circuit since the gap is reduced. The resulting graph has one extra cycle. If the reads meet or overlap, split $q=\alpha\beta$ with $\alpha$ and $\beta^{-1}$ both readable from the base. At their respective endpoints $v,w$, choose fixed paths $p_v,p_w$ from the base. Then $q\in H p_vp_w^{-1}H$, and adjoining $q$ gives the folded identification of $v$ with $w$.

For each of the two AK(3) subgroups, no identification of a single pair of distinct vertices gives the ambient rose. The whole rank-two census then implies that every $q\notin H$ increases the rank to three. In this case the natural map $H*\langle q\rangle\to\langle H,q\rangle$ is an isomorphism, since it is a surjection between free groups of rank three. In particular, every such subgroup $H$ is malnormal. An ordinary path starting at the marked pair and using only conjugators from one fixed $\langle H,q\rangle$ cannot reach a free basis of $F$: all its words remain in that proper subgroup. This conclusion allows arbitrary interleaving and length within the prescribed subgroup of conjugators.

## Obstruction to factorization

For either pair, the endomorphism $\phi$ taking the two basis generators to the relators has unimodular abelianization, so it is injective. If $\phi=\alpha\beta$, the two factors also have unimodular abelianizations and are injective. Two proper factors would give strict intermediate inclusions $\operatorname{im}\phi<\operatorname{im}\alpha<F$. The census excludes this. A composition of automorphisms and elementary self-exponent-one replacements could therefore have only one proper factor. Such a factor fixes a basis letter, so its image contains an ambient primitive. An ambient automorphism preserves this condition. The primitive-free cores rule it out for both pairs. The same reasoning allows the self-exponent negative-one replacements.

The absence of an intermediate same-rank subgroup persists after appending any number of isolated generators to the marked endomorphism. In the stabilized core, each added generator contributes just one labelled loop at the basepoint. Every proper same-rank overgroup would again be a principal quotient by the preceding abelianization argument. Each added label still appears only as its basepoint loop after folding, so any such quotient splits as an old rank-two overgroup with the added free factor. The only possibilities remain the original image and the ambient rose. This rules out two proper factors in every stabilized rank. The rank-two exclusion of a single elementary factor uses ambient primitiveness in that rank.

These statements concern factorizations of specified endomorphisms. Ordinary Andrews–Curtis moves can change the generated subgroup by conjugating one relator, so this is a restriction on one approach to the conjectures.
